# Other worlds — concept prompts (M8)

Prompts for an image generator (ChatGPT) to see each world before building it, the same way as the Wren:
**step 1** shows four directions for a world on one sheet; you pick one (or mix: "B's ground with D's ruin");
**step 2** turns the chosen one into a sheet to build from; then a short decision table, and building starts
on your "start building".

## How to use

1. Start a new ChatGPT chat for each sheet and **attach two images** to every prompt:
   - `docs/images/solenne-meadow.jpg`: our game's look (the style to match)
   - `docs/concepts/wren-chosen-sky-whale.jpg`: the Wren (so the ship stays the same)
2. Paste one prompt below as it is. Five sheets: Lull, Cinder, Verran, Orrin's moons, the giants.
3. Save each result into `docs/concepts/` as `world-lull-sheet.png`, `world-cinder-sheet.png`,
   `world-verran-sheet.png`, `world-moons-sheet.png`, `world-giants-sheet.png`, and tell me the letters
   you like (one per world, or a mix).

What the worlds already are in the game (seen from afar, M2/M7) is written into each prompt, so the
pictures match the sky and the far views: sizes, colours, what hangs in each sky.

## Step 1 — Lull

```
Concept-art sheet: four directions for one alien world, arranged in a 2×2 grid in one image, each panel labelled only with a large letter A, B, C, D. No other text.

Art style: match the first attached image (a screenshot of our game) — hand-painted animated-film concept art, soft painterly shading, gentle light, muted colours, clean readable silhouettes, quiet and full of wonder, never grim or military. Original designs — do not copy or resemble any existing film, game or space photo.

Scale, the same in every panel: the second attached image is our ship, the Wren. Draw this exact ship (same rounded patched hull in cream, terracotta and teal, glass dome, short wings with glowing engine pods) landed on its four legs in the middle distance. It is about 26 m long and 10 m tall. A small figure in a simple rounded pale spacesuit with a glass helmet stands by its ramp. Same camera in every panel: a wide landscape view from a little above head height, the sky filling the top third.

Terrain must be big and readable — craters, ridges, hills, cliffs — so it can be generated by code from noise; no fine clutter. Anything built is made of simple chunky shapes (domes, cylinders, arches, rings).

The world: LULL, the big moon of our home planet Solenne. Airless, low gravity (one sixth of Earth's), fine grey dust and old craters. True in every panel:
- Black sky with stars even by day. A dazzling white sun, about five times as wide as our sun looks from Earth, casting long shadows.
- Our home planet Solenne hangs in the sky, about three times as wide as Earth's full moon looks to us: a green world patched with big white cloud banks and a soft blue rim of air. It never moves in Lull's sky.
- The horizon is close and gently curved (Lull is small).
- Pale warm grey highlands (#a8a49c), dark grey lava plains (#4f4d4a), bright fresh crater rays (#dcd8cf); shadows are soft painterly blue-grey, never pitch black.

The four directions:
A — "Silent highlands": rolling rounded grey hills and soft old crater rims, smooth dust, a few big boulders, Solenne high above.
B — "Shore of the dark sea": the edge of a dark lava plain with low wrinkle ridges, and a winding collapsed lava channel leading away to a cave mouth in its wall.
C — "Rim of a young crater": on the terraced rim of a fresh crater with bright rays and a central peak inside.
D — "The old observatory": an ancient domed observatory and a ring-shaped landing cradle of the vanished civilisation (smooth pearl-white ceramic and brass, softened by a million years of dust), half buried, facing Solenne.
```

```

The four directions:
A — ORRIN, "Cloud canyons": towering amber and cream cloud cliffs and canyons lit by a low sun, the Wren flying between them.
B — ORRIN, "Under the rings": just above the cloud tops, the rings arching across the whole sky as a vast bridge of light, their shadows striping the clouds below.
C — VAEL, "The green deep": soft layered sea-green haze, cloud decks stacked like a calm sea, glittering ice crystals and a halo around the small sun.
D — VAEL, "Ringlets": high above the clouds near the edge of night, the thin ringlets as sharp silver arcs, the planet's curve below fading into deep teal night.
```

## Step 2 — the chosen direction, to build from

For each world, once you have picked: attach that world's sheet (and the Wren image), replace `[WORLD]` and
`[LETTER]`, and paste.

```
Using direction [LETTER] from the attached sheet of [WORLD], make a second sheet in exactly the same style and colours, arranged 2×2, labelled only ORBIT, LANDING, GROUND, LANDMARK:
- ORBIT: the whole world seen from space as a globe, lit from one side, with what hangs near it.
- LANDING: the same landing site as [LETTER] at a different time of day (low sun, long shadows), the Wren and the suited figure for scale.
- GROUND: four close-up swatches side by side: bare rock, the loose surface (dust, sand, ice or glass), a single big boulder, the edge of a small crater or crack.
- LANDMARK: the landmark from this world's sheet (or the most striking feature, if there is none) in a clean side view on a plain background, the suited figure beside it for scale.
For the giants, GROUND shows four cloud and haze types instead, and LANDMARK a clean view of the rings or the great storm.
```

# The Wren — concept prompts (M4)

Prompts for an image generator (ChatGPT) to see the ship before building it.

**v1** (`wren-v1-sheet.webp`): the art style was right, but the ships were too small — the user wants a
ship you can walk around inside like a house (not huge), old-looking but clearly very advanced.
**v2** below is the current prompt. Step 1: four directions on one sheet. Step 2: a cutaway of the chosen
design to plan the walkable interior. Step 3: a turnaround to build the exterior from.

## Step 1 — four house-sized versions on one sheet (v2)

```
A concept-art sheet showing four different designs for the same spaceship, arranged in a 2×2 grid in one image, each panel labelled only with a large letter: A, B, C, D. No other text.

Art style: the same hand-painted animated-film concept art look as a warm, nostalgic fantasy film — soft painterly shading, gentle late-afternoon light, clean readable silhouettes, cosy and whimsical, never military. Original design — do not copy or resemble any existing film, game or toy spaceship.

The ship is "the Wren": a home-ship that one pilot, a cat and the ship's AI live aboard. SIZE IS IMPORTANT: it is as big as a two-storey house — about 30 metres long and 10–12 metres tall — big enough to walk around inside like a home, but not a giant ship. Two decks, shown by two rows of round glowing windows. A wide boarding ramp is lowered to the grass and the open hatch shows a warm lamp-lit interior (a glimpse of shelves, a rug, a lamp). A person standing at the foot of the ramp reaches only about a sixth of the ship's height; a small cat sits on the ramp.

It looks old on the outside but is very advanced inside — built by a civilisation far beyond ours, then flown and repaired for centuries:
- Old-fashioned outside: rounded hull of riveted panels in slightly different colours, faded painted stripes, patches, a little moss and lichen in the seams and on the roof, brass trim, a large glass dome over the cockpit.
- Quietly futuristic underneath: smooth pearl-white ceramic engine parts showing between the old panels; thin lines of soft blue-white light along the seams; engines that glow softly instead of smoking (no smokestacks, no propellers); the landing legs have clean, precise mechanisms and the feet hover a hand's width above the grass on a faint glow; a soft holographic glow on the instruments inside the dome; one round lamp near the nose glows calmly like an eye (the ship's AI).

Palette: muted cream (#efe2c8), warm terracotta red (#a4553f), aged brass (#a17c46), weathered teal-green (#3f6f62), dark wood brown (#4a3424), soft meadow greens, with pearl-white tech parts and small accents of soft blue-white light.

Setting, identical in all four panels: parked on a green grassy meadow on a high plateau, a sea of white clouds below and behind it, a pale blue sky with a few soft cumulus clouds, late-afternoon sun from the left. Same camera in every panel: three-quarter front view from a little above eye level, the whole ship in frame with the person and cat clearly visible for scale.

Shapes must stay simple and chunky — rounded cylinders, spheres, boxes and capsules — so each design could be modelled from basic primitives; no fine greebles.

The four directions:
A — "Sky whale": a big, plump, whale-like hull with a rounded nose and two decks of portholes, a glass observation dome on the back, broad stubby wings with glowing engine pods underneath.
B — "Lighthouse": a taller, upright home-ship — a tower-like bridge at the front with a wraparound band of windows and the dome on top, a wide lower deck behind it, ring-shaped glowing thrusters under the belly.
C — "The wren": a large bird-like body with a raised tail fin, short swept wings with engine nacelles on their tips, the cockpit dome placed like an eye at the front.
D — "Sky yacht": a long hull like an old airship gondola, a covered balcony walkway with a brass railing along one side, a large glass bow, two engine pods that can tilt.
```

## Step 2 — cutaway of the chosen design (to plan the interior)

Replace `[LETTER]` and paste with the chosen image attached.

```
Using design [LETTER] from the attached image, draw a cutaway side view of this exact ship with the near hull removed, showing its two decks as a cosy lived-in home, in the same hand-painted style and colours. Label the rooms in small neat hand lettering: COCKPIT (under the glass dome, two worn seats and glowing holographic instruments), GALLEY AND TABLE, BUNK ROOM, HALDEN'S CORE (a calm softly glowing sphere in a brass cradle), WORKSHOP, CARGO HOLD (by the ramp), ENGINE ROOM (smooth pearl-white machinery with soft blue-white light), and the stairs or ladder between the decks. Show the person and the cat inside for scale. Flat, even lighting; clean side-on view.
```

## Step 3 — turnaround of the chosen design (to build the exterior)

```
Using design [LETTER] from the attached image, make a clean model-sheet turnaround of this exact ship: front view, left side view, rear view and top view, arranged in one image on a plain light background, all at the same scale, with the person and cat for scale beside the side view. Keep the same colours, patches, portholes, dome, wings, engines, landing legs and ramp. Flat even lighting, no perspective distortion, no text except small labels FRONT, SIDE, BACK, TOP.
```
